# Quality Checklist

Run this audit before delivering the notes.

## Source Integrity

- [ ] The response states exactly what was read.
- [ ] It does not claim full-book coverage from excerpts or previews.
- [ ] Unknown metadata is marked `[待核]`.
- [ ] Notes, appendices, tables, and references are not assumed to have been read unless available.

## Attribution

- [ ] Author statements are separated from `[归纳]`, `[推断]`, and `[延伸]`.
- [ ] No theory is attributed merely because vocabulary resembles that theory.
- [ ] The author's opponents are represented fairly and specifically.

## Argument Reconstruction

- [ ] The analysis reconstructs reasoning rather than retelling section order.
- [ ] Major claims include evidence and warrants.
- [ ] Missing inferential steps are identified.
- [ ] Correlation is not silently converted into causation.
- [ ] Case evidence is not silently generalized to a universal conclusion.

## Quotations

- [ ] Every quotation is verbatim from the source.
- [ ] Every quotation has a verified page number or a justified `[页码待核]`.
- [ ] Paraphrases do not appear in quotation marks.
- [ ] Original-language quotations are preserved; translations are labeled.

## Critical Evaluation

- [ ] Praise and criticism point to concrete arguments or evidence.
- [ ] Each major limitation includes textual basis and a plausible alternative.
- [ ] Confidence labels evaluate the analyst's criticism and include reasons.
- [ ] Blind spots are relevant rather than mechanically imposed.

## Research Reuse

- [ ] The output identifies how the source can be used in a literature review.
- [ ] Reusable concepts, claims, methods, and cases are clearly separated.
- [ ] Further questions emerge from genuine unresolved tensions.
- [ ] The final one-page card accurately compresses, rather than distorts, the analysis.

## Style

- [ ] Language is precise, restrained, readable, and non-inflationary.
- [ ] Empty claims such as “具有重要意义” are replaced by specific explanations.
- [ ] The author's self-declared contribution is not treated as automatically proven.
- [ ] Sections unsupported by evidence are omitted or marked, not invented.
